About FatFatBaby: 1 in 8 Billion Life Simulator

Welcome to FatFatBaby.com, an interactive birth-lottery and reincarnation life simulation browser game inspired by real global demographic distributions. Every second, roughly four babies are born somewhere on Earth. In this game, you step into the shoes of a brand-new infant drawn from global birth probabilities across developing and developed nations, urban megacities, and rural villages.

How the Global Birth Lottery & Reincarnation System Works

  • Weighted Global Draw: Your birthplace, family socioeconomic tier, and baseline healthcare access are rolled at Age 0 using population-weighted probabilities—meaning you are much more likely to begin life in high-birthrate regions like India, Nigeria, Indonesia, or Brazil than in Monaco or Iceland.
  • Annual Life Focus & Crossroads: Guide your character year by year by choosing whether to prioritize Study & Skill, Work & Hustle, Self-Care, or Family & Friends. At major turning points, pause time to make high-stakes life decisions.
  • Four Parallel Lives: Watch four other infants born in the exact same second grow up alongside you in real time, highlighting how geography and luck interact with personal choices.
  • Reincarnation Karma & Village of Lives: Every completed life is enshrined in your browser's local Village of Remembered Lives and grants Karma points that unlock blessings for your next incarnation.
