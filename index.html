How to Play FatFatBaby Snack Catcher

Welcome to FatFatBaby.com! Help the hungry baby catch falling donuts, burgers, and cookies while dodging spicy chili peppers. Built for instant browser play on both mobile and desktop with zero downloads required.

Controls & Tips

  • Desktop: Move your mouse left and right inside the game screen.
  • Mobile: Drag your finger across the canvas to slide the basket.
  • Scoring: Every sweet snack gives +10 points. Missing a snack or touching a chili resets your combo!
