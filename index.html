About FatFatBaby.com — Kawaii Multi-Language Life & Career Simulator

Welcome to FatFatBaby.com, an interactive birth-lottery browser game styled in soft pastel Japanese comic artwork. Choose how fast your 80-year life unfolds—from a 2-minute blitz to a 5-minute standard game or a 15-to-30-minute cozy desk simulation. Watch your companion on the right side of the screen evolve from a 1-year-old infant into adulthood with country-specific cultural backdrops and career outfits, then export a collectible PNG character card to share with friends.
